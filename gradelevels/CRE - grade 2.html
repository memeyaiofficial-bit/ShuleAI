📖 Five Main Topics

What We Learn in CRE – Grade 2

Our faith journey includes Creation, The Holy Bible as a guide, The Early Life of Jesus, Christian Values, and The Church with the Holy Spirit.

01 🌌 Topic 1

Creation

Discover God's wonderful creation! Learn about self-awareness, the importance of family, and how God created the sky, sun, moon, and stars.

🧒 Self-awareness 👨‍👩‍👧 My Family ☀️ Creation of the Sky, Sun, Moon & Stars
▶ Play Now
02 📖 Topic 2

The Holy Bible

The Bible as our guide in daily life. Explore the divisions of the Bible, the call of Samuel, Daniel in the den of lions, and the faith of the widow of Zarephath.

📜 A Guide in Daily Life 📚 Divisions of the Bible 🕯️ The Call of Samuel 🦁 Daniel in the Den of Lions 🍞 The Widow of Zarephath
▶ Play Now
03 ⭐ Topic 3

The Early Life of Jesus Christ

Follow Jesus from His birth, the kindness of the Wise men, calming the storm, the miraculous catch of fish, healing the man with a withered hand, and the miracle of Easter.

🎄 Birth of Jesus Christ 👑 Kindness of the Wise Men 🌊 Jesus Calms the Storm 🐟 Miraculous Catch of Fish ✋ Healing the Withered Hand 🌸 Easter
▶ Play Now
04 💖 Topic 4

Christian Values

Grow in godly character by learning and practicing important Christian values: sharing, obedience, honesty, thankfulness, forgiveness, responsibility, and the value of work.

🤝 Sharing 🙇 Obedience 💎 Honesty 🙏 Thankfulness 💕 Forgiveness ⚙️ Responsibility 🛠️ Work
▶ Play Now
05 ⛪ Topic 5

The Church

Understand the Church as a family of believers. Learn about the power of Prayer and the wonderful gift of the Holy Spirit who guides and comforts us.

🙏 Prayer 🕊️ The Holy Spirit

💪 Skills You'll Gain

Deepening Faith & Character

In Grade 2, you will grow deeper in Bible knowledge, Christian values, prayer life, and understanding the Holy Spirit's role — skills for a faithful life!

📖
Bible Knowledge — Understand divisions of the Bible and key stories (Samuel, Daniel, Widow of Zarephath).
💖
Christian Values — Practice sharing, honesty, forgiveness, responsibility, and the dignity of work.
🕊️
Holy Spirit & Prayer — Develop a deeper prayer life and learn about the Holy Spirit as our helper.
🌊
Miracles of Jesus — Learn about Jesus' power over nature, healing, and the resurrection at Easter.
