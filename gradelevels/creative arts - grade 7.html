🎯 Five Core Topics – Grade 7

What We Learn This Year

Our Grade 7 Creative Arts journey covers five comprehensive areas that deepen your understanding, practical skills, and critical engagement with arts and culture.

01 ✨ Topic 1

Introduction to Creative Arts

Explore the purpose, forms, and history of creative arts. Examine visual arts, music, dance, drama, and their connections to identity, culture, and modern society. Develop a personal artistic philosophy.

🎭 Art Movements 🖼️ Mediums & Styles 💡 Creative Thinking 🌍 Global & Kenyan Art 🧠 Art & Identity
▶ Explore Module
02 🖌️ Topic 2

Drawing & Painting

Master advanced drawing techniques: perspective, shading, proportion, and composition. Experiment with acrylics, watercolors, ink, and mixed media. Create expressive works inspired by Kenyan landscapes, figures, and abstract concepts.

✏️ Realistic Sketching 🎨 Color Theory & Harmony 🖌️ Acrylic & Watercolor 🏞️ Landscape & Portraiture 🖼️ Abstract & Symbolic Art
▶ Art Studio
03 ⚽ Topic 3

Sports & Creative Movement

Investigate how sports connect with creative arts through rhythm, coordination, expression, and storytelling. Develop choreographed movement sequences, team routines, and explore sports as performance art.

🤸 Advanced Gymnastics Flow 💃 Choreography & Story 🏀 Sports Performance Arts 🎵 Rhythmic & Musicality 🏅 Leadership & Collaboration
▶ Move & Create
04 🎵 Topic 4

Kenyan Folk Songs

Study and perform Kenyan folk songs from various ethnic groups: Luo, Kikuyu, Luhya, Kamba, Mijikenda, and more. Learn polyrhythms, call-and-response, composition, and the cultural stories behind each song.

🎶 Vocal Technique 🥁 Indigenous Instruments 📜 Historical Context 🎤 Composition & Arrangement 🇰🇪 Cultural Preservation
▶ Sing Kenya
05 🔍 Topic 5

Analysis of Creative Arts

Develop advanced skills to analyze, interpret, and evaluate creative works. Use art criticism frameworks: describe, analyze, interpret, judge. Discuss context, meaning, technique, and emotional impact in visual art, music, dance, and performance.

👁️ Visual Analysis 📝 Critical Writing 🎭 Performance Evaluation 💬 Aesthetic Theory 🔎 Comparative Studies
▶ Analyze Art
