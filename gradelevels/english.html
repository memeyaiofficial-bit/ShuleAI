What We Learn in English

Our English journey covers five exciting areas that build a strong foundation in speaking, reading, writing, and communication!

01 👋 Topic 1

Greetings, School & Family

Learn how to greet people politely, talk about your school life, and introduce your family members. Build confidence in everyday English conversations!

02 🏠 Topic 2

Home, Time, Weather & Environment

Explore your home, learn to tell time, describe the weather, and understand the world around you. Discover how to talk about your daily life!

03 🧍 Topic 3

Hygiene, My Body & My Friends

Learn about personal hygiene, name the parts of your body, and talk about your friends. Build healthy habits and strong friendships through English!

Safety, Community Leaders & Living Together

Understand how to stay safe at home and school, learn about community leaders who help us, and discover the importance of living together peacefully.

Technology, Numbers & Conservation
