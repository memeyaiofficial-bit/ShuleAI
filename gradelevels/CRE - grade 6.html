📖 Five Core Themes - Grade 6

What We Learn in CRE – Grade 6

Our Grade 6 journey explores God's covenants with His people, the messages of the prophets, Jesus' teachings in depth, the birth and growth of the Church, Christian ethics, and the empowering work of the Holy Spirit.

01 🌍 Topic 1

Creation

Discover the wonder of God's creation! Learn about the biblical account of creation, the order of creation days, humanity as God's image-bearers, and our responsibility to care for the earth as faithful stewards of God's beautiful world.

📖 The Creation Story 🗓️ Six Days of Creation 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Made in God's Image 🌿 Stewardship of Creation 🙏 Praising the Creator 💚 Caring for God's World
▶ Play Now
02 📖 Topic 2

The Bible

Understand the Bible as God's inspired Word and our guide for life. Explore its structure — Old and New Testaments, the books of Law, History, Poetry, Prophecy, Gospels, and Letters. Learn how to read, interpret, and apply Scripture to daily living.

📚 Old & New Testaments 📜 Books of Law & History 🎵 Poetry & Wisdom Literature 📢 Major & Minor Prophets ✝️ The Four Gospels 💌 The Apostles' Letters 🙏 Reading & Applying Scripture
▶ Play Now
03 ✝️ Topic 3

Life & Teachings of Jesus

Dive deep into Jesus' life — His birth, baptism, teachings (Sermon on the Mount), powerful parables, miracles that reveal God's kingdom, His passion, death, and glorious resurrection.

👶 Birth & Early Life 💧 Baptism & Temptation 🏔️ Sermon on the Mount 📖 Parables of the Kingdom ✨ Miracles of Jesus ✝️ Passion & Death 🌟 Resurrection & Ascension
▶ Play Now
04 🕊️ Topic 4

The Early Church

Understand the coming of the Holy Spirit at Pentecost, the birth and growth of the early church, the apostles' teachings, fellowship, breaking of bread, prayer, and Paul's missionary journeys.

🔥 Pentecost & Holy Spirit ⛪ Life of the Early Church 🕊️ Apostles' Teaching 🙏 Fellowship & Prayer 🚢 Paul's Missionary Journeys 📜 Letters to the Churches
▶ Play Now
05 💖 Topic 5

Christian Ethics & Service

Grow in Christian character through the Fruits of the Spirit, discover your spiritual gifts, learn to make ethical decisions, practice forgiveness, justice, and serve others in love.

🍇 Fruits of the Spirit 🎁 Gifts of the Spirit ❤️ Love in Action 🙏 Forgiveness & Reconciliation ⚖️ Justice & Fairness 🤝 Serving Others 🌍 Christian Leadership
▶ Play Now

💪 Skills You'll Gain – Grade 6

Deepening Faith, Wisdom & Leadership

In Grade 6, you will grow in biblical understanding, covenant knowledge, prophetic insight, deeper knowledge of Jesus' life and teachings, church history, Christian ethics, and the empowering work of the Holy Spirit.

📖
Biblical Literacy — Understand God's covenants and the messages of the prophets.
✝️
Christ-centered Living — Apply Jesus' teachings, parables, and example to daily decisions.
🕊️
Spiritual Discernment — Recognize the fruits and gifts of the Holy Spirit.
⛪
Church & Leadership — Understand the early church model and develop servant leadership skills.
💖
Christian Ethics — Make ethical decisions based on biblical values and serve others.
