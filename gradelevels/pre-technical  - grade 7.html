📐 Five Core Topics – Grade 7

What You'll Learn in Pre-Tech

Our Grade 7 Pre-Technical Studies curriculum builds foundational skills in drawing, materials, measurement, and entrepreneurship — preparing you for the world of technology and innovation!

01 🔧 Topic 1

Introduction to Pre-Technical Studies

Discover what pre-technical studies is all about! Learn about the importance of technology in our daily lives, explore different technical fields, and understand safety practices in the workshop environment.

⚙️ What is Pre-Technical Studies? 🏭 Technology in Daily Life 🛡️ Workshop Safety Rules 🔬 STEM Career Pathways 🧰 Tools and Equipment Overview
▶ Play Now
02 📐 Topic 2

Introduction to Drawing

Master the basics of technical drawing! Learn about drawing tools (pencils, rulers, set squares), line types, sketching techniques, and how to create accurate technical sketches for design projects.

✏️ Drawing Tools & Equipment 📏 Line Types (Thick, Thin, Hidden) 📐 Using Set Squares & Protractor 🖍️ Sketching Techniques 📄 Orthographic Projection Basics
▶ Play Now
03 🧱 Topic 3

Introduction to Materials

Explore the different types of materials used in technology and construction. Learn about wood, metals, plastics, ceramics, composites — their properties, uses, and how to select the right material for a job.

🪵 Wood: Hardwood & Softwood ⚙️ Metals: Ferrous & Non-Ferrous 🧪 Plastics: Thermoplastics & Thermosets 🏺 Ceramics & Composites ♻️ Material Properties & Selection
▶ Play Now
04 📏 Topic 4

Measuring and Marking Out Tools

Learn to use precision measuring and marking tools accurately! Discover how to use rulers, tape measures, calipers, try squares, marking gauges, and scribers for accurate layout work in projects.

📏 Linear Measurement (Rulers & Tapes) 📐 Squares (Try Square, Combination Square) 🔧 Calipers & Vernier Scales ✏️ Marking Gauges & Scribers 🎯 Accuracy & Precision in Marking
▶ Play Now
05 💡 Topic 5

Introduction to Entrepreneurship

Discover the world of entrepreneurship! Learn what it means to be an entrepreneur, how to identify business opportunities, basic business planning, marketing, and financial literacy for young innovators.

💼 What is Entrepreneurship? 🔍 Identifying Business Opportunities 📋 Business Planning Basics 📢 Marketing & Customer Needs 💰 Financial Literacy for Young Entrepreneurs
