🗂️ Five Core Topics – Grade 6

What We Learn This Term

Our Grade 6 Creative Arts journey covers five exciting areas that help you become a confident, expressive, and skilled young artist!

01 🎨 Topic 1

Creating & Executing

Bring your artistic visions to life using advanced techniques! Learn to plan complex artwork, choose professional-grade materials, and create unique masterpieces — from detailed paintings and drawings to sculptures, ceramics, and mixed media installations.

🖍️ Advanced Drawing & Sketching 🖌️ Professional Painting Techniques ✂️ Craft & 3D Design 🏺 Advanced Sculpting & Pottery 🎨 Mixed Media & Installation Art 💡 Creative Project Planning 🖼️ Digital Art Basics
▶ Play Now
02 🎭 Topic 2

Performance & Display

Share your artistic talents with confidence and professionalism! Learn advanced stage presence, theatrical production, music performance, dance choreography, and exhibition curating. Showcase your work to real audiences through school events and art shows.

🎤 Advanced Performing Arts 🖼️ Professional Art Exhibition 🎬 Script Writing & Storytelling 💃 Advanced Choreography 🎵 Music Performance & Ensemble 🎭 Stage Presence & Directing 📸 Portfolio Presentation
▶ Play Now
03 🌟 Topic 3

Art Appreciation & Critique

Develop a sophisticated eye for beauty and meaning in art! Learn formal analysis techniques, art history movements, cultural perspectives, and how to give and receive constructive feedback. Understand the role of art in society.

👁️ Formal Art Analysis 💬 Descriptive Critique Methods ❤️ Emotional & Contextual Response 🌍 Global Art Movements 🤝 Peer Feedback & Reflection 📜 Art History Overview 🏛️ Museum & Gallery Studies
▶ Play Now
04 🔍 Topic 4

Analysis of Creative Arts

Develop critical thinking skills through in-depth analysis of creative works! Learn to examine and interpret visual art, music, theatre, and dance. Understand artistic elements, principles of design, cultural context, and meaning-making in creative expression.

🔍 Elements of Art & Design 📊 Principles of Composition 🎭 Analyzing Theatre & Performance 🎵 Music Analysis & Interpretation 💃 Dance Critique Methods 🌍 Cultural & Historical Context 📝 Writing Art Critiques 🏛️ Comparative Art Studies
▶ Play Now

💪 Skills You'll Gain – Grade 6

Growing as a Creative Artist

Creative Arts builds advanced creative thinking, professional collaboration, artistic communication, self-confidence, digital literacy, and music theory — skills for high school and beyond!

🧠
Advanced Creative Thinking — Generate original ideas and solve complex creative problems with professional techniques.
🤝
Collaboration & Leadership — Work effectively in creative teams and lead group art projects and performances.
💬
Artistic Communication — Express complex ideas through multiple art forms and articulate constructive critiques.
⭐
Professional Confidence — Present your work publicly, build a portfolio, and celebrate artistic achievements.
💻
Digital Literacy — Use digital tools for graphic design, photography, video editing, and music production.
