Level 1: The Call of the Disciples

Discover how Jesus called ordinary people to follow Him and become fishers of men. Learn what it means to answer Jesus' call in your life today!

Follow Me Discipleship Faith Response New Life
"Come, follow me," Jesus said, "and I will send you out to fish for people." - Matthew 4:19
Early one morning by the Sea of Galilee, Jesus saw two brothers, Simon Peter and Andrew, casting their nets into the lake. They were fishermen, working hard all night with no catch. Jesus walked up to them and said with authority and love: "Come, follow me, and I will send you out to fish for people." Immediately, they left their nets and followed Him!
What do you think it means to "fish for people," and how can you follow Jesus' call in your life today?
